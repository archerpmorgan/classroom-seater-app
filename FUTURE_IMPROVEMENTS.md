# Future Improvements for Seating Chart Generator

## Recently Completed ✅

1. **Click-to-Swap Student Mode** - Instead of dropdown selectors, users can now click two students directly to swap their positions. Toggle swap mode, click first student (shows blue ring), click second student, and they swap automatically.

2. **Delete Desk Mode** - New toolbar button (trash icon) allows clicking on any desk to remove it from the chart. Useful for customizing layouts by removing unwanted desks. Changes are saved to history for undo.

3. **Privacy Mode Default** - Privacy mode is now enabled by default when loading the app, hiding sensitive student information (skill levels, languages, compatibility data) during screen sharing.

4. **Full Student Data Swap** - When swapping students in the seating chart, all student data (name, language, skill level, compatibility info) correctly follows the student to their new position. This works because students are referenced by ID and looked up from the master student list.
